Find the cause before changing anything.

- Reproduce the problem first, with a failing test where you can, and say how you reproduced it.
- Trace it to the root cause and state the cause in a thought before you edit any code. If you cannot reproduce it, say what you tried and stop to ask instead of guessing at a fix.
- Make the smallest fix that removes the cause, and keep the reproducing test.
- Finish the way the delivery instructions below say, and put the cause and the fix in your final reply.
